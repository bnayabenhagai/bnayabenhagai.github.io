Privacy Policy

Email Connector Claude is a private tool used only by its owner, Bnaya Ben Hagai, on his own Google accounts.

The tool reads Gmail messages and Google Drive files of the owner's accounts in order to sort and file his own invoices and documents. Data is processed only by the owner's own automation tools, at his request, and files are stored only in his own Google accounts.
